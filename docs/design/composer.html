notemap the routing composer reference

Every state the composer has. One line holds the place; what will happen is read off it and said in one word. There is no do step. Taking a destination splits it into two columns — the line, the word and the tree on the left, everything consulted or settled after it on the right, ending in the commit.

A mockup, not a component. No state, no interaction, no data. What must survive is the system — one line, the hierarchy shown rather than walked, a state as an inverted word, red for an action and nothing else.
